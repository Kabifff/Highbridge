Ham radio (APRS), for licensed amateurs:
- See and message APRS stations over the internet (APRS-IS)
- Go on the air through a KISS TNC: Bluetooth LE or classic, USB, or Direwolf on Wi-Fi; works with LoRa APRS trackers
- Position beacons and status, with map symbols
- Mesh gateway: licensed Meshtastic and MeshCore users reach APRS under their own call sign
- Automatic station ID in Meshtastic licensed mode
- Grid squares and call sign lookup
