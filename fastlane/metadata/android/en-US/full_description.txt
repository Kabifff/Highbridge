<p><b>Highbridge</b> talks to Meshtastic and MeshCore LoRa radios from one app, and can bridge public channels between the two networks. It was made in the West 7th neighborhood of Saint Paul, Minnesota, and works on every LoRa band in the world.</p>

<p><b>Chat and the bridge</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Channels and direct messages on Meshtastic and MeshCore radios, over Bluetooth, USB-C or Wi-Fi</li>
<li>Bridge mode copies public channel messages from one mesh to the other, tagged with where they came from. Direct messages are never bridged, and loops are blocked</li>
<li>Small photos, Codec2 voice notes and live push-to-talk, with automatic resend of missing pieces</li>
<li>Replies, emoji reactions, @mentions, quick replies, search, delivery receipts, per-chat alerts and replies from the notification or Android Auto</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Radios and regions</b></p>
<ul>
<li>All 27 Meshtastic regions and MeshCore presets, with each region's power and duty-cycle limits</li>
<li>Airtime meter that warns before you pass your region's legal limit</li>
<li>Encrypted channels shared by QR code or link</li>
<li>MeshCore repeater admin: login, status, remote commands and path editing</li>
<li>All Meshtastic settings and modules, remote admin of other nodes, .cfg profile backups, and the MQTT client proxy</li>
<li>MeshCore Discover, regions and config backups</li>
<li>USB terminal, UF2 and ESP32 flashing, ESP32 Wi-Fi and Bluetooth updates, nRF52 Bluetooth updates, and firmware release checks</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Ham radio</b></p>
<ul>
<li>APRS over the internet or on the air through a KISS TNC (Bluetooth, USB or Direwolf), including LoRa APRS trackers</li>
<li>APRS messaging, position beacons and a gateway that lets licensed mesh users reach APRS under their own call sign</li>
<li>Meshtastic licensed mode with automatic station ID, grid squares and call sign lookup</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Field tools</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Offline-capable map of nodes, node list with signal and battery, compass to any node</li>
<li>Wardriving: map where Meshtastic and MeshCore reach, shared to a live public coverage map</li>
<li>Traceroute, topology graph, range test with a heatmap, and GPX track recording</li>
<li>Shared map pins, traceroutes on the map, and nine map views including satellite, hybrid and terrain</li>
<li>AR camera view of nodes, pins and landmarks, with a pin drop straight ahead</li>
<li>Coverage prediction from antenna height, power and gain, and a line-of-sight check over real terrain to any node</li>
<li>Camera rangefinder for distance and height</li>
<li>One-tap check-in and SOS, geofence alerts, barometer forecast, Morse keyer, ATAK position bridge</li>
<li>Power profiles that save battery, with an automatic switch at low battery</li>
<li>Automations: scheduled broadcasts, keyword replies, alerts when a node comes back, webhooks and IFTTT</li>
<li>Ringtones for your radio's buzzer, 12 accent colors, optional haptics, and a Wear OS watch app (Google Play edition)</li>
<li>Optional PIN lock and encrypted backups</li>
<li>Home-screen widget; English, Spanish and Norwegian</li>
</ul>

<p>Everything is free: no paid tiers, no add-ons to unlock. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Messages travel radio to radio and never pass through a server, unless you turn on MQTT or APRS over the internet. Wardriving coverage is shared only if you choose to.</p>

<p>This edition covers Meshtastic and MeshCore. A full edition that adds Reticulum (Sideband, MeshChat, NomadNet) is on the project's GitHub releases page.</p>
